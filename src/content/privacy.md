# Privacy

Introspectum Limited, registered in the Isle of Man, runs this site and is responsible for the data described here. Write to [{email}](mailto:{email}) with any question about it.

## What the site stores

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. Cloudflare hosts it; to deliver the pages, Cloudflare processes your IP address and the details of each request, as described in [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Waiting lists

When you join a product's waiting list, we store your email address, your company name if you give one, the product, the sentence you agreed to and the time you joined. We use them only to contact you about testing that product. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). The entries are stored with Cloudflare.

We keep an entry until the product's waiting list closes or until you withdraw your consent, whichever comes first. To withdraw it, write to [{email}](mailto:{email}); we then delete your entry.

## Email

When you write to us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, and to correct, delete or restrict it. You can object to its use and ask for it in a portable format. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the Isle of Man, the Information Commissioner; in the European Union, the authority of your country.
