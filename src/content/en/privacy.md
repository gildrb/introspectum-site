# Privacy

Fabian Klopfer runs this site for Introspectum Limited & is responsible for the data described here. His postal address is in the [imprint](/imprint). Write to [{email}](mailto:{email}) with any question about your data.

## What the site stores

The site sets no cookies & uses no analytics or tracking. Cloudflare hosts it; to deliver the pages, Cloudflare processes your IP address & the details of each request, as described in [Cloudflare’s privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Waiting lists

When you join a product’s waiting list, we store your email address, your company name if you give one, the product, the sentence you agreed to & the time you joined. We use them only to contact you about testing that product. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). The entries are stored with Cloudflare.

We keep an entry until the product’s waiting list closes or until you withdraw your consent, whichever comes first. To withdraw it, write to [{email}](mailto:{email}); we then delete your entry.

## Contact form

When you send the contact form, we store your name, your email address, your company name if you give one, the size of your team, the topic you chose, what you tell us you are building with AI, the language of the page & the time you sent it. We use them only to answer your inquiry & to prepare an offer if you ask for one. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); where your message is not about a contract, it is our legitimate interest in answering the messages sent to us (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The messages are stored with Cloudflare.

Each message is deleted automatically one year after it arrives. If it leads to a contract, we keep what the contract needs for as long as the law requires.

## Email

When you write to us, we use your message & address only to answer you.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, & to correct, delete or restrict it. You can object to its use & ask for it in a portable format. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one where you live.
